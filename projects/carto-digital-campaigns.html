CARTO — Digital Campaigns & Paid Media

As CARTO's marketing-focused designer, I developed and adapted campaign creative across a global B2B marketing ecosystem. My role combined visual concept development with the production systems needed to scale a campaign across channels, placements, formats and audiences while keeping the brand consistent.

One campaign idea, built to scale

Campaign work rarely ended with a single key visual. I worked from a core creative direction and translated it into a flexible system that could support different messages, formats and digital touchpoints. The goal was to preserve recognisable campaign language while adjusting hierarchy, composition and information density to suit each placement.

Depending on scope, a campaign could expand from a small set of core assets into dozens of final deliverables once different ratios, message variants, static and motion executions, channel needs and regional adaptations were included.

Adapting creative for paid media

The same visual idea needs to behave differently across channels. A wide display placement may require a reduced message and stronger focal point, while a square or vertical social placement can carry more context and needs a composition designed for mobile viewing. Rather than treating adaptation as simple resizing, I reworked hierarchy and layout so the creative remained clear and recognisable in each format.

For this portfolio case, selected CARTO campaign concepts can be extended into representative paid media families for Google Display, LinkedIn and Meta. These adaptations demonstrate how I approach format constraints and campaign consistency without implying media-buying or campaign-management ownership.

Production at scale

To keep a high volume of creative consistent and efficient, I relied on reusable templates, component libraries, shared visual rules and structured source files. This made it possible to move quickly between campaign work, product launches, reports, events, partner marketing and web deliverables without rebuilding the visual language from scratch each time.

I managed multiple concurrent requests and feedback cycles while maintaining the quality and consistency of the wider CARTO brand. This system-based approach was especially useful when a campaign needed to expand quickly into multiple formats or when a visual direction had to be reused by other members of the marketing team.

Global brand, different audiences

CARTO operated internationally, so campaign design needed to stay consistent across markets while remaining flexible enough for different audiences, stakeholders and communication needs. My role required close collaboration with marketing and regional teams, careful brand governance and fast adaptation of existing assets when messaging or campaign requirements changed.

My role

I owned the creative production side: visual direction, campaign design, adaptation, motion and brand consistency. I collaborated with marketing stakeholders responsible for campaign strategy, distribution and performance. Where analytics or campaign feedback informed new iterations, I translated those needs into updated creative rather than owning media buying, targeting or campaign setup.
